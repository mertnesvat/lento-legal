A shield doesn’t appear, or an app doesn’t lock again

Open Lento → Profile and check that Screen Time is allowed. Lento relies on Apple’s Screen Time; if it’s been turned off in Settings → Screen Time, Lento can’t protect your apps.

Managing or cancelling Lento Pro

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lento. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Restoring a purchase

On a new iPhone, open Lento → Profile → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.
